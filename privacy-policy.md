## 2. Information we collect

### Information received through Shopify

When you install and use WeightCheck, we may receive and process information from your Shopify store that is needed to provide the app, including:

- Store identification information, such as your store domain and Shopify shop ID.
- Product and product-variant information, such as product names, variant names, SKUs, product and variant IDs, inventory-item IDs, shipping requirements, and shipping-weight measurements.
- Audit configuration, scan selections, audit status, audit results, flagged weight issues, and weight corrections made through the app.
- Shopify installation, authentication, and session information needed to securely connect the app to your store.

WeightCheck is designed to inspect product and variant shipping weights. We do not need to collect customer names, customer addresses, payment-card information, order contents, or other buyer information to provide this service.

### Information you provide directly

We may collect information you provide when you contact us for support, such as your name, email address, store domain, and the contents of your message.

### Technical information

We may collect limited technical information needed to operate and secure the app, such as request timestamps, error information, browser or device information, and server or security logs. We do not use this information to track buyers across stores or websites.

## 3. How we use information

We use information to:

- Authenticate your Shopify store and provide WeightCheck’s features.
- Scan product variants for missing, zero, or suspicious shipping weights.
- Display audit results and allow you to update product-variant weights at your direction.
- Maintain, troubleshoot, secure, and improve the app.
- Respond to support requests.
- Comply with legal obligations and enforce our agreements.

We do not sell personal information. We do not use Shopify store data for advertising or unrelated marketing.
